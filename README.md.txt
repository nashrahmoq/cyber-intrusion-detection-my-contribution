# Cyber Intrusion Detection and Classification

## University Group Project

This project was completed as part of the Programming for Data Analytics
module.

The group project analysed network traffic data to investigate
relationships between network-flow characteristics and the presence
of attacks.

## My Contribution

My individual contribution focused on:

**Objective 3 — Assessing the relationship between Sbytes and Dur
towards the presence of attack.**

My work included:

- Cleaning the `Sbytes` and `Dur` variables
- Preparing the variables for analysis
- Applying data transformation
- Comparing the distributions of `Sbytes` and `Dur` between Normal
  and Attack traffic
- Analysing the correlation between `Sbytes`, `Dur`, and `attack_flag`
- Investigating the impact of traffic intensity on `Sbytes`, `Dur`,
  and attack patterns

## Analysis

### 1. Distribution Analysis

I compared the distributions of `log(Sbytes)` and `log(Dur)` between
Normal and Attack traffic using boxplots.

### 2. Correlation Analysis

I used a correlation heatmap to examine the relationships between
`Sbytes`, `Dur`, and `attack_flag`.

The analysis found a very weak negative linear relationship between
`Sbytes` and `Dur`, with a correlation of approximately -0.05.

### 3. Traffic Intensity Analysis

I investigated the relationship between traffic intensity, `Sbytes`,
`Dur`, and attack patterns.
